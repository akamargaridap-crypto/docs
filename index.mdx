## Get Started in Three Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Account">
    Sign up at [app.akapeople.ai](https://app.akapeople.ai) and log in to the Kira dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get Your API Key">
    Navigate to **Settings → API Keys** and generate your first key. You'll use it to authenticate every request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send Your First Message">
    Call the Kira API with your key and start a conversation. Check out the [Quick Start](/quickstart) guide for a full walkthrough.
  </Step>
</Steps>
